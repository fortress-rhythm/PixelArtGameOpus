# Night Bridge cast — design log

The five people in `night_bridge.html` (`src_bridge/03_people.js`). They are drawn by the scene's own code, not
the adventure's `CAST_DEFS`: the scene is a side view at 30 px with gestures the dolls don't have (catching an arm,
a cloak coming off). Their designs come from the brief (`docs/briefs/night-bridge.md`) and were started from
blank. Nothing was copied from Act I's cast or the garden's woman (both © Odiriuss).

## Who they are

- **The woman** (`her`, 30 px): secretive but unhurried. She keeps the lighthouse and comes and goes at night.
- **The older child** (`kidA`, 21 px): runs ahead, leans over railings, gets caught first.
- **The younger child** (`kidB`, 19 px): stops for fireflies, follows.
- **The innkeepers** (`keeper` 31 px, `keeper2` 28 px): an older couple, seen mostly in the doorway.

## Silhouette

- **Her:** a fitted cloak to the ankle, read as a long dress, with a low hair tail behind. Without the cloak: a
  tunic to mid-thigh over loose trousers that flare at the ankle, the cloak folded over her forearm like a bag. The
  outline changes clearly between the two (the hem goes from the ankle to mid-thigh).
- **Older child:** a knitted cap with a brim and a short jacket.
- **Younger child:** two bunches of hair and a coat to the knee.
- **Innkeeper:** broad, bald with a grey fringe and moustache, an apron over a waistcoat.
- **His wife:** a grey bun, a shawl over a long dress.

## Signature feature

Her colours. Strawberry-auburn hair, a dark red cloak, a teal and sky-blue tunic. They are palette families that
nothing else in the scene may use (a unit test checks the frames before she arrives).
